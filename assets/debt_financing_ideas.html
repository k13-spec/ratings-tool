Methodology

Ranking = blended score (0–10) = 40% near-term addressable need (refinanceable maturities next 12–24m + committed event/capex debt draw) + 25% structural size (gross debt & EBITDA scale) + 20% event probability (announced & funded vs. speculative) + 15% market fit (rating band sweet-spot for spread product + demonstrated NCD appetite). Size gate (widened Jul 2026): a company qualifies with EBITDA ≥ ₹500 cr or revenue > ₹1,500 cr together with gross debt > ₹1,500 cr — the second path pulls in mid-market credits beyond the usual large-cap suspects. There is no group-concentration screen: multiple companies from the same conglomerate are ranked independently on their own merits. All qualifying ideas are kept — cards stream in as you scroll rather than being capped.

v3 rules (Jul 2026): (1) Thermal/coal screen — companies whose business is coal/thermal power generation, coal mining or coal trading are excluded; their non-coal subsidiaries qualify in their own right (Tata Power is out, TPREL is in). Industrials using captive coal power as an input (steel, cement, aluminium) are tolerated — flag if you want this tightened. (2) Deleveraging parents — where the parent is deleveraging, the screen looks for capital-hungry verticals instead (Bharti → Nxtra). (3) PE-backed borderline names (Blackstone/Carlyle/KKR/ChrysCapital/Kedaara etc.) are included. (4) SPV pools and restricted groups are candidates in their own right where pool-level ratings clear A+.
