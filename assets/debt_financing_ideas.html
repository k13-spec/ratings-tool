Screening criteria (all applied)

EBITDA ≥ ₹500 cr Domestic rating ≥ A+ Trigger 1: gross debt ≥ ₹3,000 cr with ≥ ₹500 cr maturing in 12m or Trigger 2: acquisitive or Trigger 3: capex ≥ ₹2,000 cr upcoming Excluded: BFSI · PSUs

Watchlist — near-misses worth monitoring

Methodology

Ranking = blended score (0–10) = 40% near-term addressable need (refinanceable maturities next 12–24m + committed event/capex debt draw) + 25% structural size (gross debt & EBITDA scale) + 20% event probability (announced & funded vs. speculative) + 15% market fit (rating band sweet-spot for spread product + demonstrated NCD appetite). Toggle above to re-rank purely by estimated opportunity size.

What was reviewed per company

Last 3 years of rating rationales (CRISIL / ICRA / CARE / Ind-Ra), FY24–FY26 results and earnings-call disclosures, recent NCD/bond issuance and refinancing actions, announced M&A and capex programmes, and press coverage through early July 2026. Capital-structure and lender-base entries are tagged ⚑ inferred where they rest on typical disclosures rather than a document reviewed for this screen — verify these in the Company Deep Dive before pitching.
